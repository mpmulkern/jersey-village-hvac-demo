AC Replacement in Houston, TX

Replacing an air conditioner in Houston is not a like-for-like swap. Our cooling season is long, our humidity load is high, and a system chosen by copying the label on the old one frequently underperforms from the first week. Jersey Village HVAC Pros sizes Houston replacements from an actual load calculation, installs matched equipment, and corrects the airflow problems that shortened the life of the system you are replacing.

Quick Answer: What does a new AC system cost in Houston?

Typically $4,500 to $9,500 installed, driven by tonnage, SEER2 rating, whether the indoor coil and air handler are replaced as a matched set, and the state of the existing ductwork. The on-site estimate is free and itemised, and financing is available.

Sizing Matters More Here Than Almost Anywhere

Houston's problem is not only heat, it is moisture. An air conditioner removes humidity by running long enough for the coil to keep condensing water out of the air. An oversized unit satisfies the thermostat fast, shuts off early, and never reaches that steady state, so the house ends up cold and damp with the thermostat reading correct. Homeowners then drop the setpoint to compensate, which costs money and does not fix the feeling.

That is why we run a load calculation on the house as it is now, accounting for square footage, orientation, window area and glazing, insulation level, attic condition and duct losses. Plenty of Houston homes have had insulation upgrades or window replacement since the original system went in, which genuinely changes the answer. Matching the old tonnage just inherits whoever guessed last time.

What a Houston Replacement Includes

  • Room-by-room load calculation and honest tonnage recommendation, not a copy of the existing label
  • Matched condenser, evaporator coil and air handler so the rated efficiency is real
  • Static pressure and airflow check, with duct correction quoted separately and plainly
  • New line set or a documented flush, correct vacuum, and a weighed-in charge
  • Condensate drain, trap and secondary pan brought up to standard on attic installs
  • Free written estimate, financing options and a 10-year workmanship warranty

Houston Neighbourhoods and Housing Stock

We work across the Houston side of our coverage area, concentrated in the northwest: Spring Branch, Oak Forest, Garden Oaks, the Heights fringe, Fairbanks and the neighbourhoods inside Beltway 8 nearest Jersey Village. The older housing there brings the classic Houston constraint, which is duct space. Mid-century homes were built around smaller equipment and tighter chases, so a well-chosen modern system sometimes needs return-air work to breathe properly. We would rather tell you that during the estimate than discover it on install day.

Newer Houston construction has the opposite issue: adequate duct sizing paired with builder-grade equipment that was installed to hit a price. Those systems often reach the end of their service life around year twelve with the ductwork still perfectly serviceable, which keeps the replacement straightforward and the cost at the lower end of the range.

Repair or Replace?

If your system is under ten years old and the fault is a capacitor, a fan motor or an accessible refrigerant leak, repair is almost always right. The calculation shifts when the compressor is failing, the evaporator coil is corroded through, or the system runs R-22 and needs a costly recharge. Our repair-versus-replacement guide sets out where that line falls, and we quote both ways so the choice stays yours.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does AC replacement cost in Houston?

Most Houston replacements run $4,500 to $9,500 installed. Tonnage, SEER2 efficiency, whether the coil and air handler are replaced as a matched set, and the condition of the existing ductwork are what move the number. Every estimate we give is free, written and itemised.

Is a bigger AC unit better for Houston heat?

No, and oversizing is one of the most common mistakes we correct. An oversized system cools the air quickly then shuts off before it has removed the humidity, leaving a house that is cold, clammy and expensive to run. Correct sizing comes from a load calculation, not from matching whatever was there before.

How long does a replacement take?

A straightforward changeout on an accessible system is usually a single day. Add time if the air handler is in a tight attic, the line set needs replacing for a refrigerant change, or the ductwork needs correction as part of the job.

Do you offer financing for Houston homeowners?

Yes. Replacement almost always arrives unplanned, usually in August, so we offer financing options alongside the written estimate and every job carries a 10-year workmanship warranty on top of the manufacturer's equipment warranty.
