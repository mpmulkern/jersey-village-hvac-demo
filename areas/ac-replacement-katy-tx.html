When Replacement Beats Repair

There is no universal rule, but the arithmetic is usually clear once you have the numbers. A system past twelve years, running the discontinued R-22 refrigerant, facing a repair over about a third of replacement cost, is rarely worth fixing. So is any unit that has needed three or more service calls in two seasons. Below that threshold — a healthy eight-year-old system with a failed capacitor or fan motor — repair is straightforwardly the better value, and we will tell you so.

What we will not do is quote a replacement without first confirming the existing system genuinely cannot be economically repaired. If a technician has told you that you need a new system without diagnosing the actual failure, get a second opinion before you spend the money. Our AC repair page covers what a proper diagnosis should include.

Sizing for Katy Homes

Katy's housing stock is heavily weighted toward two-storey homes built from the late 1990s onward, many with large west-facing glazing and open two-storey entries. Those features change the load calculation significantly, and they are exactly why matching the tonnage of the outgoing unit is unreliable — the original may itself have been oversized by a builder working from a rule of thumb.

We run a load calculation based on the actual house: square footage, orientation, window area and glazing type, insulation levels, ceiling heights and duct layout. In this climate the goal is a system that runs longer cycles at lower capacity, because that is what removes humidity. A unit that satisfies the thermostat in eight minutes has not dehumidified anything, which is why oversized systems leave a house feeling cold and damp at 74 degrees while the compressor cycles itself toward early failure. Two-stage and variable-speed equipment handles this considerably better than single-stage, and in a Katy two-storey it is usually worth the difference.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a new AC system cost in Katy, TX?

A full replacement in Katy typically runs $4,500–$9,500. Size (tonnage), SEER2 efficiency rating and the condition of existing ductwork drive the range. Two-storey homes needing zoning or a second system sit at the higher end. Every estimate is free and on-site.

How long does an AC replacement take?

A straight like-for-like changeout is usually one day. Add ductwork modifications, a new line set, electrical changes or a switch to a heat pump and it runs into a second day. We confirm the schedule before starting so you know how long the house is without cooling.

Is a bigger AC unit better for Katy heat?

No, and this is the most expensive mistake in Houston-area replacements. An oversized unit cools to setpoint quickly then shuts off before it has removed humidity, leaving a house that feels cold and clammy while the compressor short-cycles itself to an early death. Correct sizing comes from a load calculation, not from matching whatever was there before.
