Common Questions

How much does AC repair cost in Jersey Village, TX?

Most AC repairs (capacitor, contactor, refrigerant leak, fan motor) in Jersey Village and Houston run $150–$650. A failed compressor can cost more and may make replacement the better value. Jersey Village HVAC Pros provides a free diagnostic estimate before any work starts.

How much does a new AC system cost?

A full AC system replacement in the Jersey Village / Houston area typically costs $4,500–$9,500, depending on tonnage, SEER2 efficiency rating, and ductwork condition. Jersey Village HVAC Pros provides a free, itemized on-site estimate before any work begins, with no obligation to proceed.

Should I repair or replace my AC?

If your system is under 10 years old with an isolated issue, repair is usually the better value. If it's 12+ years old, uses R-22 refrigerant, or has had multiple recent repairs, replacement typically costs less over time. A free inspection will give you a clear recommendation either way.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency AC service?

Are you licensed and insured?

How often should I service my AC in Houston?

Twice a year is recommended: once in spring before cooling season, and once in fall. Houston's long cooling season puts more run-hours on a system than most of the country, so a spring tune-up in particular catches problems before peak demand.

What size AC unit do I need?

Correct sizing depends on square footage, insulation, window exposure, and ductwork — not a simple square-foot rule of thumb. We perform a proper load calculation on every replacement estimate to avoid an oversized or undersized system.
