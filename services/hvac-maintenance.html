HVAC Maintenance & Tune-Ups in Jersey Village, TX

Houston's long, humid cooling season is hard on an AC system. Jersey Village HVAC Pros offers seasonal maintenance and tune-up plans for homeowners across Jersey Village and the greater Houston metro, designed to catch small problems before they become a mid-summer breakdown.

What We Check on Every Visit

Refrigerant Charge

Low charge is the single most common cause of poor cooling and premature compressor wear.

Coil Cleaning

Houston's pollen and humidity load coils fast — a dirty coil cuts efficiency and airflow.

Electrical Components

Capacitors and contactors inspected before they fail during peak-demand summer heat.

Condensate Drain

A clogged drain line is a leading cause of water damage — cleared and tested every visit.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I get HVAC maintenance in Houston?

Twice a year is recommended in the Houston area: once in spring before cooling season starts, and once in fall. Houston's long cooling season puts more run-hours on a system than most of the country sees, so a spring tune-up in particular catches problems before peak summer demand.

What does an AC tune-up include?

A full inspection of refrigerant charge, electrical connections, condensate drain, coil cleanliness, and airflow, plus a filter check and thermostat calibration.

Does maintenance actually prevent breakdowns?

Most emergency no-cool calls trace back to a problem that a routine tune-up would have caught — a low refrigerant charge, a dirty coil, or a failing capacitor. Regular maintenance also protects most manufacturer warranties, which often require documented service.
