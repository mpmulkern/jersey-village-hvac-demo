When to Call for Emergency Service

Complete No-Cool

AC not producing any cold air, especially with elderly residents, infants, or pets in the home.

Electrical Burning Smell

A burning odor from the unit is a safety issue — shut it off and call immediately.

Water Leaking Indoors

A clogged condensate line or frozen coil can cause water damage if left unaddressed.

System Won't Shut Off

A stuck relay or thermostat fault that runs the unit constantly needs same-day attention.
