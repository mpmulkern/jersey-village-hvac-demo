Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a new AC system cost in Jersey Village?

A full AC system replacement in the Jersey Village / Houston area typically costs $4,500–$9,500 depending on tonnage, SEER2 efficiency rating, and ductwork condition. Jersey Village HVAC Pros provides a free, itemized on-site estimate before any work begins.

How do I know if I need a repair or a full replacement?

If your system is under 10 years old with an isolated issue, repair is usually the better value. If it's 12+ years old, uses R-22 refrigerant, or has had multiple recent repairs, replacement typically costs less over time and improves efficiency. A free estimate will give you a clear recommendation.

How long does AC replacement take?

Most residential AC replacements in the Jersey Village / Houston area are completed in a single day, from removal of the old system to startup and testing of the new one.
